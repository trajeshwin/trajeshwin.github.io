SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 September 2026. This policy covers text messages sent by Rajesh Thiru, sole proprietor, Munster, Indiana.

What this service is

A personal automated notification service. A script I wrote sends me text messages about activity on my own brokerage account: when an order fills, and when a position is left without a protective stop order.

Who receives messages

One person: me, the owner and operator of the service. There are no customers, subscribers, or other recipients. There is no public sign-up.

Information collected

One mobile phone number, my own, stored in a configuration file on a private server I control. No information is collected from any other person.

Mobile numbers are never shared

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile phone numbers are not sold, rented, or disclosed to any third party for any purpose. Information is shared only with the messaging provider strictly as needed to deliver a message.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on account activity. A typical week is between zero and ten messages. Messages are sent only during US market hours on trading days.

Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help. Messages can also be disabled directly in the sending system's configuration.
